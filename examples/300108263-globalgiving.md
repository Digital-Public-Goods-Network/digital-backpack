## Mission

Connect nonprofits, donors, and companies in nearly every country in the world,
enabling community-led solutions to address local problems.

## Theory of change

Local organizations closest to a problem are best positioned to solve it.
GlobalGiving acts as infrastructure: vetting nonprofits, providing a trusted
giving channel for individual and corporate donors, and distributing funds
globally. By aggregating small donors and large institutional giving through a
single platform, it reduces the transaction cost of international giving while
increasing accountability through community feedback loops.

## Programs

### Crowdfunding Marketplace

- **Type**: Fiscal intermediary / open platform
- **Description**: Vetted nonprofits post projects and receive donations from
  individuals and institutions worldwide. Nonprofits retain ~85% of funds after
  platform fees.
- **Scale**: ~5,000 active nonprofits across 170+ countries
- **Population served**: Communities served by vetted nonprofit partners globally
- **Outcomes tracked**: funds raised per project, donor retention, nonprofit
  retention rate, narrative reports submitted

### Corporate Giving Programs

- **Type**: Managed program services
- **Description**: Employee giving, matching gift administration, and cause
  marketing for corporate clients. Includes rapid-response disaster giving
  for corporate donors.
- **Population served**: Corporate clients and their employees; downstream
  nonprofit partners

### Disaster Relief Fund

- **Type**: Rapid-response pooled grantmaking
- **Description**: Activated during declared disasters; distributes to vetted
  local responders already on the ground rather than deploying external
  organizations.
- **Population served**: Communities affected by natural disasters and
  humanitarian crises
