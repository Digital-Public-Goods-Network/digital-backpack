## Mission

<!-- Not stated on ProPublica; inferred from organization type -->
Provide a rigorous, student-centered education in a nurturing independent school
environment on Massachusetts's North Shore.

## Theory of change

<!-- Inferred — org would fill this in themselves -->
Strong early education creates lifelong learners and engaged citizens. As an
independent school, Shore operates outside public school funding constraints,
allowing flexibility in curriculum, pedagogy, and community culture. Tuition
revenue is supplemented by endowment and philanthropy to support financial aid,
ensuring access is not limited entirely by ability to pay.

## Programs

### Core Academic Program

- **Type**: Direct educational services
- **Description**: Full-day instruction across grades pre-K through 8, including
  core academics, arts, athletics, and extracurriculars.
- **Scale**: ~300 students (estimated; org would confirm)
- **Population served**: Children on Massachusetts's North Shore, primarily
  grades pre-K through 8
- **Outcomes tracked**: Student academic progress, graduation/secondary school
  placement, standardized assessment results
